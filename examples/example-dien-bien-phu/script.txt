In the spring of 1954, a French army waited in a remote valley in northwest Vietnam. It was confident, well supplied, and certain that no enemy could bring heavy guns over the mountains.

It was wrong.

# The Guns in the Hills

For weeks, tens of thousands of porters pushed artillery up jungle slopes by hand. Sometimes a single gun moved only a few hundred meters in a night. Why would anyone attempt something so impossible?

{tense} On March 13, the hills opened fire. Within hours, the outpost of Him Lam had fallen. [pause 1.6]

The French artillery commander, unable to silence the guns, took his own life… The siege had begun.

# The Last Days

On May 7, Vietnamese soldiers raised their flag over the French command bunker. More than ten thousand prisoners began the long march to the camps.

Today, the hills are quiet. But the trenches are still there.
